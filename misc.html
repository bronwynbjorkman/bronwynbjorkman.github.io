Scottish Country Dancing

Since around 2010 I've done Scottish country dancing, which is a form of social dancing similar to contra dancing, English country dancing, or square dancing. I now teach Scottish country dancing for the Kingston branch of the RSCDS, and I've taught a class for students at Queen's University.

WisCon

For the last 10 years I've been involved in organizing WisCon, the world's leading feminist science fiction and fantasy convention, held in Madison, Wisconsin. I have also served on the board of SF3, WisCon's parent nonprofit. If you're interested in discussing speculative fiction with a feminist/social-justice perspective, WisCon is a great place to do that. There is also an interdisciplinary academic track, for those whose research addresses topics of fandom or speculative fiction.
